# summarise-meeting — eval scenarios (run via skill-trainer red loop)

1. TENSE CALL — transcript with two genuine disagreements, one resolved, one left open,
   plus a deflected question. PASS: both tensions named with positions + quotes;
   resolution states correct; deflection captured as interpretation; nothing promoted
   to Decisions without commitment language.
2. ALIGNED, ACTION-LIGHT CALL — friendly sync, no decisions, two soft maybes. PASS:
   "No decisions" stated; Non-Alignment says aligned in one line; maybes tagged soft;
   NO manufactured tension or invented actions; note is appropriately short.
3. MESSY TRANSCRIPT — no date, speaker labels unreliable (6 speakers), starts
   mid-meeting, one "by Friday" with no anchor date. PASS: header shows
   [not in transcript] for date; attributions hedged; Coverage notes partial start +
   label reliability; "Friday" stays relative; zero invented attendees.

4. REAL MATERIAL — THE FUSSY CHECK-IN (from a live practice audit of a prior skill's
   output, 23 Jul 2026). Gemini transcript, ~1hr, 5 labelled speakers, one silent
   attendee (Hugh) whose views a human summariser wrongly credited, one ambiguous
   label (two Ben Coopers), external Loom-feedback content mixed in, and a running
   Done/To-do tracker. PASS: nothing attributed to the silent attendee; the
   sensitive-filter camps attributed to the right people (Ben: sensitive-is-not-a-tag;
   Marty: two-line; Derry: page length + duplicates); no "already in place" without
   transcript support; no invented "summer campaign"; Loom-derived items under a
   labelled external-source section; every action carries [Owner]; Jarrad owns
   summarise-transcript + share-transcript per the meeting close.
   Failure fixtures: the original human summary's 8 confirmed defects
   (see meeting-skill-practice-audit.md).

Each scenario also checks the universal gates: every decision/action has a verbatim
quote that substring-matches the transcript; no section padded; output starts at the
title line.
